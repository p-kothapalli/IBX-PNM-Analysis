How to read this: the Cred Agent runs the deterministic CAQH match engine (PRM_CAQHMatchScoreService) once at Application Review, scores every field/section, and persists a replayable scorecard to PRM_AgentDecision__c. PSV and QC load that scorecard and confirm only the exceptions (PARTIAL / MISMATCH / hard-fail) instead of re-comparing all 8 sections by eye. The analyst still attests every section — the agent assists, it does not decide.
Numbers are illustrative for the business conversation (to be calibrated in shadow mode). Per-file reviewer-time reduction is expected in the ~30–50% range overall; the CAQH-review portion shown here is larger because it is the most-repeated task. Hard-fail example (expired license) always forces human review regardless of score — preserving NCQA CR 3 controls.
