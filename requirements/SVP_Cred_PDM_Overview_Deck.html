Two more cred motions: cyclical recred + on-demand off-cycle

Re-Credentialing — cyclical, every 2–3 years

  • 3 OmniScripts — ReCredUpdate, ReCredQCUpdate, RecredQC
  • Driven by 4 scheduled batches — due-date check, CAQH access check, notification email, letter generation
  • CAQH re-validation is a hard gate — record cannot advance without a valid attestation
  • Failure path recently hardened — letter fallback & silent-failure monitoring shipped
  • Drives the bulk of cred volume — the engine that keeps the network compliant

Off-Cycle Credentialing — event-driven

  • 3 OmniScripts — OffCycleCredentialing, OffCycleVerification, OffCycleQCReview
  • Abbreviated PSV and accelerated QC for exception circumstances
  • Lower volume but high importance — the “exception lane” for special situations
  • Same audit trail as initial cred — no compliance shortcuts

Three layers of review keep credential decisions defensible

1 · Primary Source Verification (PSV)

  • Independent verification against authoritative sources — NPDB, state license boards, education registrars. 4 OmniScripts incl. dedicated NPDB sub-flow (PRM_PSVSubOsWSNPDB).

2 · Quality Control (QC)

  • 10 QC OmniScripts spanning manual updates, off-cycle, non-routine, HACAC, and delegated practitioners. Every cred and PDM decision passes through a QC checkpoint.

3 · HACAC Committee Review

  • Non-routine cases land here (PRM_NonRoutineCommitteeReview, PRM_ReviewHACAC). Output flows back into cred records and adverse action logs — full audit trail by design.

Where the platform hurts today

01

High-volume submissions hit governor limits

Practitioners with 3+ practice locations × 3+ taxonomies × 15+ networks generate 54+ records and routinely exceed Salesforce's 150-DML / 60s-CPU ceiling.

Result: 5-min UI freezes, silent partial saves, 15–20 tickets/wk, 2–3 hrs remediation per ticket.

02

Capitated Bundle search caps at 500

Search returns at most 500 of 1,582 active bundles. The validation rule "PL is in a capitated bundle" actually checks CAP program participation — wrong field, misleading message.

Blocks legitimate billing-address updates for affected HCFs.

03

Edit-in-flow not supported

PSV / Cred reviewers see a wrong field (DOB, address, education) and have to exit the flow, edit the record, restart. Tied to a separate 4 MB Save-for-Later payload limit.

Slow, error-prone, biggest hit on large practitioners.

All three are quantified, root-caused, and actively being unwound — see slide 12 for the roadmap.
