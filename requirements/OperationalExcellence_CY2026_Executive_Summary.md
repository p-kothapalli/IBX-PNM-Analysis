# Operational Excellence — 2026 Year-to-Date

**Provider Data Management & Credentialing**
**Period:** 1 January – 31 August 2026
**Source:** Production data, extracted 31 August 2026

---

## Headline

**92,006 requests were completed** over the eight months — the volume of work the teams got through,
including requests that arrived in late 2025 and finished this year.

Looking only at the work that arrived in 2026:

| | Requests | Work steps |
|---|---:|---:|
| Arrived in 2026 | 107,686 | 203,356 |
| Now complete | 71,128 (66%) | 162,003 (80%) |
| Still open | 36,558 | 41,353 |

**Nine in ten open work steps have never been started** — 37,052 of 41,353 are untouched and only 639 are
actively in progress. **28.5% of all requests are now created automatically.**

A request is one submission a specialist works. A work step is one review task inside it; a request
generates one to four.

## Are we keeping up?

**Yes.** The first quarter fell behind by roughly 17,000 requests. From April the teams held break-even, and
June and August reduced backlog outright. Incoming volume also fell 41% from the February peak.

This is a queue-capacity problem, not a throughput problem — the work is not moving slowly, it is not being

These figures count work steps created between January and August 2026 that were still open on 31 August.
Items created before January are excluded, so this is the unfinished remainder of this year's work, not
total organisational backlog.

## Effort is concentrated in Credentialing

| Team | Requests | Share | Work steps | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Provider Data Management | 70,755 | 65.7% | 72,280 | 38.6% |
| **Credentialing** | **36,931** | **34.3%** | **114,993** | **61.4%** |

Credentialing handles a third of the requests and nearly two thirds of the actual work. **Any capacity or
headcount decision based on request counts alone will under-resource it by roughly half.**

PAR Form is the extreme case: 4.0 work steps per request, generating 58,163 steps from just 14,502 requests
— nearly the effort of Provider Data Change, which carries four times the volume. **Seven specialists handle
it.**

Automation now originates 30,576 requests (28.5%): scheduled processes generate all Re-Credentialing and
Ancillary Re-Assessment volume, and system integrations generate 9,268 Provider Data Changes and 2,158 PAR
Forms.

## Four request types need a different measure

For most types a request is marked complete when the work finishes. For four, that final step is not
recorded, so the reported rate understates performance.

| Request type | Reported complete | Work steps finished | What is really happening |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Off-Cycle | 204 (10%) | 3,809 of 6,262 (61%) | 1,663 of 2,057 requests stop at Network Management QC, which acts as the final stage in practice. |
| PNC | 17 (1%) | 1,687 of 3,446 (49%) | Same pattern — 768 held at Network Management QC, 360 at Application Review. |
| Ancillary Assessment | 31 (24%) | 195 of 322 (61%) | 75 held at PDA Review and Update. Low volume, inconsistent close-out. |
| Ancillary Re-Assessment | 134 (31%) | 28 of 427 (**7%**) | **A genuine backlog, not a recording gap** — 399 of 427 stalled at source verification, 395 awaiting application materials. |

The first three are a reporting problem; the work is being done. Closing out the request when the final
review finishes, or adopting work-step completion as the official measure, would resolve it. Ancillary
Re-Assessment needs operational attention — 92% of its volume has not moved past source verification.

## Quality

**79 of 92,006 completions were reopened and redone — under one tenth of one percent.** Off-Cycle accounts
for 48 of them, 19% of its 252 completion events, and is the only figure worth reviewing.

---

## Automation and IT follow-up

**20 scheduled processes are active, 3 paused.** In the most recent measurable week automated processing ran
**9,918 times with a 100% success rate**.

1. **Three paused processes need a decision.** Two have been dormant since mid-2025 and one has never run
   since being set up in June 2025. They cover future-dated address and provider ID activation — if that
   function has not been replaced, those activations have not processed for over a year.
2. **2,928 provider network link records failed and were never retried**, all after 22 May 2026 and 95% from
   a single process. The effect is gaps in provider network data.
3. **Error monitoring is currently unusable.** Since 20 March one process has logged a normal condition as
   an error, generating roughly 440,000 false records a month — about 97% of the year's total. Genuine
   failures are buried at around 1,000 to 1. No figure in this report is affected, but system problems would
   not currently be detected.

One account is credited with 10,194 Provider Data Changes, 17% of that type and 2.6 times the next-highest
individual. This is most likely a shared or bulk-processing account and should be confirmed before the
figure is used in any individual comparison.
